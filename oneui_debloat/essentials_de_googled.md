# 🍃 De-Googled Essentials (FOSS+)
*Privacy-respecting, open-source replacements for the standard Google/OEM suite, featuring specialized tools for India.*
---

### 📱 Communication & System
* **[Fossify Phone](https://github.com/FossifyOrg/Phone)** ([F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.fossify.phone/)): A clean, ad-free dialer with no tracking and smooth performance.
* **[Fossify Contacts](https://github.com/FossifyOrg/Contacts)** ([F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.fossify.contacts/)): Manage your people locally without syncing data to third-party servers.
* **[Fossify Messages](https://github.com/FossifyOrg/Messages)** ([F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.fossify.messages/)): Simple SMS/MMS management with robust blocking features.
* **[Mull](https://f-droid.org/en/packages/us.spotco.mull/)**: A privacy-hardened fork of Firefox that strips out telemetry and trackers.
* **[Cromite](https://github.com/uazo/cromite)**: A de-googled Chromium fork with built-in ad-blocking and enhanced privacy.

### 📅 Productivity & Organization
* **[Etar](https://github.com/Etar-Group/Etar)** ([F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/ws.xsoh.etar/)): A minimalist Material Design calendar for local or DAVx5 synced data.
* **[Fossify Calendar](https://github.com/FossifyOrg/Calendar)** ([F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.fossify.calendar/)): Fully private scheduling with custom reminders.
* **[Quillpad](https://github.com/quillpad/quillpad)** ([F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/io.github.quillpad/)): A powerful "Google Keep" alternative with Markdown support.
* **[Notally](https://github.com/omgodse/notally)** ([F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.omgodse.notally/)): A minimalist, high-speed note-taking app stored purely on-device.
* **[Fossify Clock](https://github.com/FossifyOrg/Clock)** ([F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.fossify.clock/)): Lightweight alarm, stopwatch, and timer.

### 📂 Files & Media
* **[MiXplorer](https://mixplorer.com/)**: (Proprietary/Ad-free) Arguably the most powerful and customizable file manager for Android. Includes built-in servers (FTP/HTTP), archive support, and cloud integration.
* **[Material Files](https://github.com/zhanghai/MaterialFiles)** ([F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/me.zhanghai.android.files/)): An open-source file manager with root support and clean UI.
* **[Aves Libre](https://github.com/deckerst/aves)** ([F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/deckers.thibault.aves.libre/)): Superior gallery for exploring metadata and massive photo libraries.
* **[Fossify Gallery](https://github.com/FossifyOrg/Gallery)** ([F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.fossify.gallery/)): Fast, offline media management with a built-in editor.
* **[Unitto](https://github.com/sadellie/unitto)**: A combined calculator and unit converter that surpasses standard system apps.

### 🗺️ Navigation & India Hiking Resources
* **[Trail Sense](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kylecorry.trail_sense)**: An all-in-one survival tool with a compass, barometer, and weather tools for offline use.
* **[Organic Maps](https://github.com/organicmaps/organicmaps)** ([F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/app.organicmaps/)): Privacy-focused, lightning-fast offline maps based on OpenStreetMap.
* **[OsmAnd+](https://f-droid.org/en/packages/net.osmand.plus/)**: Professional-grade navigation with detailed contour lines and hillshading.
* **[Bhuvan (ISRO)](https://bhuvan.nrsc.gov.in/)**: India’s official satellite and 2D/3D geoportal for detailed terrain data.
* **[Survey of India](https://onlinemaps.surveyofindia.gov.in/)**: Access official **Open Series Maps (OSM)**—the gold standard for trekking in India.
* **[Incredible India Adventure](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/experiences/adventure)**: Centralized resource for state-recognized trekking routes and operators.
* **[Himalayan Treks (Uttarakhand)](https://uttarakhandtourism.gov.in/page/explore-adventures)**: Official route maps and permission guides for the Garhwal and Kumaon regions.

### ☁️ Cloud & Sync
* **[Proton Drive](https://github.com/ProtonDriveApps/android-app)**: End-to-end encrypted cloud storage to replace Google Drive.
* **[Nextcloud](https://f-droid.org/en/packages/com.nextcloud.client/)**: The ultimate tool for self-hosting files, contacts, and calendars.
