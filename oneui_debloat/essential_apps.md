# Essentials
*Replace inconsistent OEM stock apps with these for a uniform  feel across any device.*

### 📱 Communication & System
* **[Phone by Google](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.dialer)**: Standardizes your dialer with powerful spam protection and a clean UI.
* **[Google Contacts](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.contacts)**: Unified contact management to prevent OEM sync duplicates.
* **[Google Messages](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.messaging)**: Brings RCS and a consistent SMS experience to all devices.
* **[Gboard](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.inputmethod.latin)**: The gold standard for typing, multilingual support, and clipboard management.

### 📅 Productivity & Tools
* **[Google Chrome](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.android.chrome)**: High-speed browsing with seamless cross-device tab syncing.
* **[Google Calendar](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.calendar)**: Reliable scheduling that integrates with Gmail and Tasks.
* **[Google Keep](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.keep)**: Fast, synced notes and checklists.
* **[Google Clock](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.deskclock)**: Minimalist, ad-free clock with Bedtime mode.
* **[Unitto](https://github.com/sadellie/unitto)**: A "superior" open-source calculator and unit converter (over 570 units supported).
* **[Google Calculator](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.calculator)**: Simple, effective, and supports Material You themes.

### 📂 Files & Media
* **[Files by Google](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.nbu.files)**: Efficient file management with cleaning tools and a "Safe Folder."
* **[Google Photos](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.photos)**: Best-in-class AI search and media backup.
* **[Google Gallery](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.photosgo)**: Lightweight, offline-first organization for media.
* **[Google Drive](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs)**: Cloud storage that integrates directly into the system file picker.

### 💳 Payments & Security
* **[Google Wallet](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.walletnfcrel)**: Standardizes payments and digital tickets, replacing brand-specific payment apps.
